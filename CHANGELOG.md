# Changes

## 1.3.1 (Oct 20, 2026)

- Settings: I forgot my current PIN lets you set a new PIN while you are signed in, without the old one.

## 1.3.0 (Oct 20, 2026)

- View as Roman: a button in Doug's Settings shows the app exactly as Roman sees it. An orange bar stays on top with Back to Doug. It is look only, so nothing can be changed as Roman.
- Reset Roman's PIN from Doug's Settings. It also clears a lockout.
- The garage car is removed for now.

## 1.2.0 (Oct 20, 2026)

- Roman's Wish List, shared by Doug and Roman, with Birthday, Christmas and Anytime ideas sections. Any item can have a link. It shows as an Open link chip.
- Home shows a countdown to Christmas and to Roman's birthday, with how many wish ideas are waiting for each.
- Roman's week: a progress bar for his chores, Sunday to Saturday, with a goal marker (80% to start). Doug sets the goal percent and the reward. When Roman hits it the card says Earned, and Doug taps Paid out. Past weeks are listed.
- Idea bank: about 55 fun things to do, picked by season. Need an idea? fills the reward box. Home shows an idea for the week for Doug. Doug can add his own ideas.
- Garage on Home with a car that has flip up headlights. Tap it or tap Pop up!
- Not in this version yet: photos in the garage, Roman adding ideas, removing custom ideas, excused days.

## 1.1.0 (Oct 20, 2026)

- Swipe a row left to get a trash can. Tap it to delete, with Undo. Roman only gets it on items he added.
- Slide items up or down with the grip (three lines) on the right of each row. Sub items slide inside their parent. Item details also has Move to top and Send to bottom.
- Tap the flag on a row to change priority: normal, high, low.
- Move an item to any other section or list from its details. Its sub items go with it.
- Move a whole section, with everything in it, to another list using the Move to menu in the section header.
- Put under: turn an item into a sub item of another item in the same section, or back into a normal item.
- Two shopping lists, Food and Other, each with its own stores in Settings. Everything already on Shopping stays in Other.
- List buttons are stacked in a grid, all the same size, so nothing slides off the side.
- Adding an item shows it right away, dimmed, then confirms. A slow connection no longer looks like nothing happened. Tapping Add twice adds once.
- Small tap feedback on buttons (Android vibrates, iPhone 17.4 and newer gives a tick when opened from the Home Screen).
- Roman's phone opens on Chores, and his lists are Roman and Dad (with a "let Dad know" line) and Roman's To do.

## 1.0.0 (Oct 18, 2026)

- First shared version: Personal, Work, Roman and Dad, and Roman lists, chores for Roman, shopping by store, PIN sign in, and one list on every phone.
